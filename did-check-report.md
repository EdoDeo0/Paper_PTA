# DiD check — Paper_PTA (Environmental Provisions in Chinese PTAs)
Design detected: **staggered adoption, continuous/dose treatment** (`WB_EP_Depth`/`TREND_EP_Count`, ~14 effective agreements 2000–2015, 3 within-destination "switchers") realized through a **triple-difference / interaction estimator** (green & dirty vs. neutral products, within firm–destination–year), not a canonical binary CS/SA staggered design · 2026-07-23 · files scanned: `New/Paper/draft_paper.tex`, `New/ROADMAP.md`, `New/Code/16–31_*.R`, `New/Code/stata/17–19b_*.do`

## Verdict in one line
The paper is unusually disciplined on **inference** (WCB + permutation + leave-one-out, honestly reported even when inconvenient) but has a structural gap on **staggered-timing identification**: the main coefficient (eq. 1, Table `tab:main`) is a continuous-treatment TWFE-style interaction with no Goodman-Bacon decomposition, no Callaway-Sant'Anna/dCDH-style estimator on the *level* specification, and no Rambachan-Roth sensitivity bounds — a referee trained on this checklist will go straight for Step 7/9 and Part D.

## 🔴 Will be attacked first
1. **No staggered/continuous-treatment-robust estimator behind the headline coefficient (Step 7 / D1).** Table `tab:main`'s main number comes from static TWFE with continuous, staggered-onset treatment. Sun-Abraham is run only on a derived destination-level composition-gap proxy, never on the primary spec, and Goodman-Bacon is cited three times as motivation but never actually run (no `bacondecomp`, no weight plot anywhere). The `fdt`-absorption defense is plausible but asserted, not tested.
2. **No sensitivity/breakdown-value analysis (Step 9), and no explicit "no bite" acknowledgment (B1).** Pre-trends are shown flat but never bounded à la Rambachan-Roth. Separately, the paper cannot show a first-stage "bite" by its own design logic (level effect is explicitly unidentifiable) — a real structural gap, self-undercut somewhat by the fact that the saturation-ladder argument implies *something* real is happening at the level that just can't be isolated.
3. **Covariate discipline fails the EPV rule outright, and Steps 3–5 (rollout plot, raw-cohort plot, covariate rationale/balance table) are essentially all absent (🔴 MISSING).** The smallest treatment cohort is **1 country** (Pakistan 2007, New Zealand 2008, Costa Rica 2011), giving an EPV budget of under 1 covariate, yet the "with controls" specification uses 3 (MFN tariff, HHI, antidumping) with no balance table anywhere in the diagnostics. Combined with Step 1 (no estimand stated before Eq. 1) and no PO-form parallel-trends statement (C1), this cluster of gaps is what a referee trained on this checklist will hit first — before even reaching the inference battery, which is the paper's strongest asset.

## 🟡 Worth shoring up
- Add a cohort×adoption-year table (data already exists in `New/Output/Diagnostics/B_treatment_entry.csv`) and a rollout/panelview plot — cheap, currently absent.
- Add a raw (unadjusted) outcome-by-cohort plot before the event-study section, to visually back the text's own claim that the late green drift is an early-cohort artifact.
- Add one sentence stating the estimand in potential-outcomes form before Eq. (1), and reframe the cell-size weighting as a deliberate per-unit-vs-per-person choice, not just a Jensen-bias fix.
- Either aggregate controls to the agreement level (~14, EPV budget ≤2) or explicitly reframe the 3-covariate "with controls" spec as non-identification-bearing robustness, and add a balance table.
- Label the bundling analysis (§4.1) explicitly as a "null-mechanism" argument, and the destination-trend pre-treatment detrending (§4.8) explicitly as the paper's real falsification exercise (it is stronger than the placebo-provision test and currently undersold as such).
- Discuss anticipation (signature vs. entry-into-force dates) explicitly, at least for the "first substantial" agreements (NZ 2008, Switzerland 2014, Korea 2015).
- State explicitly whether ASEAN's 11-destination bloc dominates the pooled coefficient (an ASEAN-exclusion check already exists in `tab:robust` — frame it as the answer to the aggregation-weighting question, D2).
- Apply the SA correction directly to the headline event-study figure (`fig:es`), not only to the derived composition-gap proxy, and add explicit "falsification, not proof" framing language for pre-trends.

## ✅ Solid
- Inference discipline (C2): destination clustering + WCB + permutation, run as three independent checks because clusters are few, not as a formality.
- Honesty and framing (C3): the dirty-margin narrative was rewritten mid-project when new results emerged, with disagreeing tests reported side by side rather than the more convenient one being selected.
- Event-study baseline (Step 8/B2): correctly pinned at $t=-1$, explicitly framed as falsification not proof.
- Magnitude benchmarking against the closest comparable paper (Brandi et al. 2020) is done carefully and in both directions (green and dirty margins), with explicit caveats about why the samples differ.

## What this audit cannot judge
- Whether the `fdt` fixed-effect argument for neutralizing staggered-TWFE contamination is *actually* sufficient in this continuous-triple-diff setting — this is a genuine open econometric question (the standard Goodman-Bacon/Callaway-Sant'Anna results are derived for binary, not continuous, staggered treatment) and would benefit from either a formal proof/citation or a simulation, neither of which this audit can produce.
- Data-quality issues upstream of the paper (HS concordance choices, green/dirty code lists) — these are addressed in `ROADMAP.md` but are a data-construction question, not a DiD-design question.
- Whether reviewers at the target journals (World Development / JEEM per the ROADMAP's own framing discussion) will weight Part A Step 7/9 gaps as disqualifying or as "nice to have" — journal norms vary on how strictly the CS/SA-descended checklist is enforced for continuous-treatment triple-diff designs.
