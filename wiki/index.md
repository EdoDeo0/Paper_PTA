# Wiki Index — Paper_PTA

## Preferential Trade Agreements & Firm Heterogeneity

- [[Baccini2017_DistributionalConsequencesPTAs]] — Firm-level evidence that US preferential tariff cuts disproportionately boost trade-related sales of the largest, most productive MNC affiliates, and increase MNC market concentration in partner countries
- [[CrowleyHanPrayer2021_DeepPTAMarkups]] — Deep PTAs reduce firm markups by ~3% (pro-competitive); competitors' PTA access reduces own exports by 7.9% per 10% share; tariff puzzle resolved via within-origin market share mechanism; 13-country EDD
- [[LefebvreFernandesRocha2021_SPSTBTFirm]] — PPML evidence that SPS/TBT provisions in PTAs strongly benefit small firms (exports +45–93%) while large firms are unaffected; harmonization and transparency channels dominant; Chile, Colombia, Peru 1996–2015
- [[LeeRochaRuta2021_TradefacilitationGVC]] — Trade facilitation provisions in PTAs increase Peruvian GVC firms' export participation by 1.8–9.9%; non-discriminatory spillovers confirmed; main channel is domestic border efficiency
- [[NeriLaine2023_DeepTradeAgreements]] — PPML evidence that deeper RTAs boost exports on average (+3.6%), but the effect is positive only for large/GVC firms and negative for small firms, consistent with a pro-competitive selection effect
- [[NeriOreficeRuta2021_GeorgiaRTA]] — OLS panel evidence that 10% more RTA depth raises Georgian firm exports by 0.46%; large firms gain +1.1%, small firms –1.2%; works through non-iceberg fixed costs

## Gravity Estimation & Methodology

- [[LarchShikherYotov2025_GravityRecommendations]] — Fifteen recommendations for gravity equation estimation: PPML preferred, use consecutive-year panel, domestic trade flows, asymmetric pair FEs, disaggregated data, and modern DiD methods

## Environmental Regulations & Trade Competitiveness

- [[DechezleprêtreSato2017_EnvRegCompetitiveness]] — Literature review: environmental regulations have small but real pollution-haven effects on trade and investment; robust positive effects on green innovation; Porter hypothesis receives partial support

## RTAs & Multilateral Liberalization

- [[Freund2010_RTAsThirdCountry]] — Latin American RTA panel: no evidence of trade diversion; preference margins predict lower future MFN tariffs (+1pp margin → –0.3pp MFN next year), supporting the building block hypothesis

## School Starting Age & Relative Age Effects

- [[BedardDhuey2006_PersistenceEarlyChildhoodMaturity]] — International evidence that relative age at school entry persistently affects test scores through grade 8 and university enrollment across 19 OECD countries
- [[BlackDevereux2011_TooYoungToLeaveNest]] — Separates school starting age from age-at-test effects using Norwegian military IQ data; finds small pure SSA effects on cognition and earnings penalties fading by age 30
